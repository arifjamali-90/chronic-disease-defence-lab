# Chronic Disease Defence Lab

Online lecture and activity site for **Nutrition and Chronic Diseases** (Nutrition & Health, Bachelor of Food Biotechnology / Food Science, FST, USIM).

**Open the lab:** https://arifjamali-90.github.io/chronic-disease-defence-lab/

## What students do

The lecture covers the immune system and nutrition, then risk factors, cardiovascular disease, hypertension, type 2 diabetes and cancer. Values follow the Recommended Nutrient Intakes for Malaysia (RNI 2017).

1. **Section 1: The immune system** (video, 9 min), then **Activity 1: Defend the Body**, a pathogen-wave quiz.
2. **Section 2: Risk factors, CVD, hypertension and diabetes** (video, 10 min), then **Activity 2: Risk Factor Rush**, a timed sorting game.
3. **Section 3: Cancer, processing, herbs and genes** (video, 5 min), then **Activity 3: Reformulation Lab**, where groups redesign a food product to meet RNI 2017 targets.

Each video must be watched to the end to unlock its activity, and captions are available. The 39 lecture slides and a summary with self-check questions are always open. The slides can also be downloaded as a PDF.

## Attendance

When a student has watched all three videos and finished all three activities, the **Attendance slip** page unlocks. The student enters their name and matric number and gets a slip with a check code. The lab sends the name, matric number, completion time, scores and check code automatically to the lecturer's Google Form ("Chronic Disease Defence Lab - Attendance"), so the attendance list builds itself in the form's Responses tab, which can be opened as a Google Sheet. Students also keep a screenshot of the slip as a backup.

To check a slip, open **https://arifjamali-90.github.io/chronic-disease-defence-lab/verify.html**, type in the details from a response or screenshot and press *Check slip*. A match means the slip came from the lab page and was not edited.

Video progress is saved in the student's browser only, so students should use the same device and browser throughout. Group summaries are copied into the course's GOALS forum.
